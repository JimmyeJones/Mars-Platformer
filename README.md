Link: https://jimmyejones.itch.io/mars-platformer

About: You are an astronaut on the surface of Mars. Your goal is to scan (jump to) all areas on the map in order to help find a suitable landing location for an upcoming mission.

Directions: Use WASD, Arrow keys, and/or SPACE to control the Martian. Attempt to explore all platforms without falling into the depths of Mars.
